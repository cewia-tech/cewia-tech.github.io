Privacy policy

Easy Altimeter

Updated September 24, 2026

Data used

The app accesses precise location while open to obtain latitude, longitude, altitude and accuracy provided by Android. On compatible devices it also reads atmospheric pressure from the barometer.

On-device processing

Location and pressure are processed on the device to calculate and display altitude. The app has no internet permission and does not transmit this data.

Stored data

Language, theme, unit, screen preference and calibration remain in the app’s private storage. There is no account, advertising, analytics or third-party sharing.

Your control

Location access can be revoked in Android settings. Preferences can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.
