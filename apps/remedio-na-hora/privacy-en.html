Privacy policy

Medicine on Time

Updated October 24, 2026

User-provided data

The app stores medicine names, optional dosages, schedules, recurrence rules, pause status, preferences, and records of taken, snoozed, dismissed, or unconfirmed doses in the device's private app storage.

Local processing

All this data is processed locally. The app does not require an account, has no internet permission, does not use advertising or analytics, and does not send or share data with CEWIA Tech or third parties.

Notifications and alarms

The app requests permission to display notifications and, on compatible Android versions, access to schedule exact alarms. These functions are used only to create local reminders at the times selected by the user. The app also receives restart, update, time, and time-zone change events to restore schedules.

Backup and retention

Automatic backup and transfer of app data are disabled. Records remain on the device until the user changes or deletes them, clears the app's data, or uninstalls the app.

Health

The app is not a medical device, does not diagnose conditions, does not recommend medicines or dosages, and does not replace advice from healthcare professionals. Confirmations represent only the actions entered by the user.

Contact and changes

Questions about this app: cewia.tech@gmail.com. This policy will be updated if the app's operation or data handling changes.
