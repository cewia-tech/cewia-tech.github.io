Privacy policy

Easy Magnifier

Updated September 23, 2026

About this policy

This policy describes data handling in Easy Magnifier by CEWIA Tech, identified as br.com.cewia.lupafacil.

Camera and local operation

The app requests camera access to display and magnify the image and control the light when available. The camera is used only while the magnifier screen is active. The app does not request microphone or location access and does not record video or audio. There are no ads, trackers, analytics, user accounts or CEWIA servers receiving images.

Frozen images and temporary files

Freeze creates a temporary capture in the app’s private storage. After loading it into memory, the app attempts to delete the temporary file. The frozen image stays in memory while you use it and is discarded when you return to the camera or the process ends. If capture is interrupted, a temporary file may remain until cleanup on the next launch. Nothing is saved to your gallery automatically.

Saving and sharing

Only when you tap Save does Android display a picker for you to choose a destination. The saved copy remains there until you delete it, including after uninstalling the app. When you tap Share, a copy is prepared in private cache and Android lets you choose a receiving app. Shared cache copies older than 24 hours are removed on the next app launch. The exported file contains the full image with the selected filter, without cropping from screen zoom. The app removes EXIF metadata during export.

Services you choose

The app works offline. If you choose a cloud destination or another app to save or share, that service may process the image according to its own policies and connection. That transfer follows your choice; CEWIA Tech does not receive a copy.

Preferences and control

Your language choice is stored locally. You can change the language, revoke camera permission in Android settings, clear app data or uninstall the app to remove private local data. Copies you saved or shared must be deleted at their respective destinations. The app does not offer its own gallery.

Contact and changes
