Easy Magnifier

Make small print bigger. Read at your own pace.

Offline · No ads · No account

Your camera, ready to help you read

Open, magnify and light up labels, instructions and small numbers. Adjust zoom using large controls or a pinch gesture, and tap the area you want to focus on.

Freeze and explore

Freeze the image, zoom and pan to read without holding your hand still. Choose Normal, Black and white or Negative for the frozen image.

You choose what to keep

Save opens the Android destination picker. Share opens available apps. Nothing is saved to your gallery automatically. The exported file includes the full image with the selected filter.

In your phone’s language

English, Portuguese and Spanish, with automatic selection and a manual option in settings.

Sharpness and live zoom depend on the camera. Lighting requires a compatible flash. Text recognition, read-aloud and translation are not included.
