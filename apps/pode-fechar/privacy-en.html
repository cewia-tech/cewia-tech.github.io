Privacy Policy – Right Bill

Last updated: August 22, 2026

PortuguêsEnglishEspañol

CEWIA Tech developed Right Bill to help users split bills and calculate tips quickly and conveniently.

Data collection

Right Bill does not collect, store, or transmit users' personal data. The app does not require registration, account creation, or login.

How the app works

All calculations are performed locally on the device. Information entered in the app is not sent to CEWIA Tech servers.

Permissions

The app does not access location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, photos, or personal files.

Data sharing

The app does not share data with third parties.

Advertising

The app does not display advertisements and does not use advertising identifiers.

Children and teenagers

The app is not specifically directed to children. It is a utility for calculating and splitting bills.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the app or legal requirements. The latest version will remain available on this page.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact CEWIA Tech at cewia.tech@gmail.com.
