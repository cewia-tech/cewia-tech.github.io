Privacy Policy

Right Bill

Application: Right Bill

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Right Bill application handles user information. Our commitment is to provide a simple, secure, and fully transparent application.

1. Data collection

The application does not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell any personal data from users.

We do not collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location
  • Photos
  • Contacts
  • Files
  • Device information
  • Usage data
  • Values entered in calculations

2. Permissions

The application does not require account creation, login, or authentication.

It also does not request sensitive permissions beyond those required for normal operation on Android.

3. Internet use

Right Bill works entirely offline. It does not depend on external servers and does not send information over the internet.

4. How calculations work

Values entered for bill splitting, service charges, and number of people are processed only locally on the device. This information is not stored by CEWIA Tech and is not transmitted to third parties.

5. Information sharing

Because no data is collected, no information is shared with third parties.

6. Security

Because the application does not store personal data, there is no storage of user information on CEWIA Tech servers.

7. Children

The application may be used by users of any age because it does not collect personal data or present inappropriate content.

8. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated in the future if new features are added. Whenever this happens, the update date will be revised on this page.
