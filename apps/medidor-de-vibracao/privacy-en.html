Privacy policy

Vibration Meter

Updated 29 September 2026

Accelerometer

The app uses the device accelerometer only while a measurement is active and the app is in the foreground. It does not access the camera, microphone, location, contacts or personal files.

Local processing

Readings are processed locally. The app has no internet permission, accounts, ads, analytics or data transmission services. It does not collect or share information with CEWIA Tech or third parties.

Stored data

Theme and language are saved in the app's private storage. Readings, chart, statistics and reference remain only in session memory and may disappear when Android ends the process. Automatic backup is disabled.

Data controls

You can reset the session to clear in-memory readings. To remove preferences, clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall it.

Contact and changes

Questions about this app: cewia.tech@gmail.com. This policy will be updated if the app's operation changes.
