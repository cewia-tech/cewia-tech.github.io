Privacy policy

A Minute for Yourself

Updated September 26, 2026

Data handled by the app

Reflections, favorites, completed moments, period views, preferences, language, and reminder times stay only in the app's private space on the device.

Transmission and sharing

The app requires no account and uses no proprietary server, advertising, or behavioral analytics. CEWIA Tech and Cristiane Tavares do not receive recorded content. Cards and PDFs are sent only after the person acts through a service they choose. The professional profile opens Instagram and is subject to that service's policies.

Permissions and reminders

Notification permission is used only for local reminders configured by the person. Reminder times stay on the device.

Retention and deletion

Automatic backup and transfer are disabled. Uninstalling deletes the records, and they are not automatically restored on another device. The app also provides an option to delete all data.

Children, changes, and contact

The app is not designed specifically for children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. This policy may change when the app's operation changes. Questions: cewia.tech@gmail.com.
