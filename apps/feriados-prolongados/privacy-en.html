Long Weekends & Holidays (Feriados Prolongados)

Long Weekends & Holidays (Feriados Prolongados)

Last updated: September 6, 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the Feriados Prolongados application, developed by CEWIA Tech, handles information while the app is being used.

2. Personal data collection

Feriados Prolongados does not require registration or login and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share users' personal data.

3. Location selected by the user

The app allows users to manually select a country and, when available, a state and city in order to view the corresponding holidays. This selection does not use GPS and does not constitute collection of precise location data. The selected preference is stored locally on the device for convenience.

4. Preferences stored on the device

Preferences such as the selected location, number of workdays considered, and color theme may be stored locally on the device. This information is used only for the operation and personalization of the app and is not sent to CEWIA Tech.

5. Offline operation

The app's main features and its available holiday database work offline. A user account is not required to use the app.

6. Data sharing

CEWIA Tech does not sell or share personal data from Feriados Prolongados users with third parties.

7. Children and teenagers

The app was not designed to collect personal information from children or teenagers and, by design, does not request registration or personal data in order to use its features.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or practices change. The current version will be made available on this page.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact CEWIA Tech at cewia.tech@gmail.com.
